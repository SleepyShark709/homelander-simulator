Additional Walk_Female, Levitate Entrance, Levitate Idle, Land_Three_Point,

contributors, CC0-1.0.
See licenses/mesh2motion-cc0.txt and .asset-source/mesh2motion/provenance.json.

All motions were retargeted offline using Auto-Rig Pro Remap and its Universal
glTF exporter. The plugin is an installed development tool and is not bundled
with the game. The original 18 clips retain their source samples.
The separate homelander-style.glb includes edited NLA compositions, phase cuts
and baked ARP head/neck constraints. Edited clips are marked explicitly in
.asset-source/homelander/style-build.json; they are not claimed as untouched
source motion or CC0 when incorporating user-supplied Mixamo animation.

The A2 standing turns additionally use Adobe Mixamo Left Turn 90 and Right Turn
90 on X Bot, downloaded on 2026-10-22 from the user's authenticated session.
Pinned FBX hashes and export settings are in
.asset-source/homelander/turns/manifest.json. Adobe's FAQ permits royalty-free
use in games: https://helpx.adobe.com/creative-cloud/faq/mixamo-faq.html
These inputs and their adapted animation are not CC0. ARP Extract Root Motion
separates the global turn trajectory; the original body performance is retained.

The relaxed A idle additionally uses Adobe Mixamo "Idle" (Standing Idle, 251
frames) on X Bot, downloaded on 2026-10-22 from the same authenticated session.
Its pinned FBX, hash and settings are in .asset-source/homelander/idle/manifest.json.
It is formally remapped as a full-body source; B and the jog start/stop edits
reuse this adapted performance. This motion is not CC0.

The laser directional locomotion additionally uses Adobe Mixamo Left Strafe,
Right Strafe and Running Backward on X Bot, downloaded on 2026-10-22.
