Input images: Image 1 is a VEHICLE DESIGN AND MATERIAL REFERENCE taken from the previously approved city environment board. Extract the relevant vehicle's visual identity as inspiration only. Create a new clean standalone object reference rather than showing or cropping a streetscape.
Style: realistic neutral PBR game-asset visualization, clear geometric forms and truthful proportions, sharp edges and readable materials, diffuse balanced studio lighting from broad lights; unlit/albedo-like colors with very restrained form shading. Avoid baked dramatic lighting, glints, strong reflections or ambient dirt that conceals geometry.
Composition: exactly ONE complete vehicle, centered, occupying about 80% of canvas width; generous empty margins on all sides so no bumper, mirror, wheel, roof or body part is cropped. A clean seamless very pale gray-white background, no horizon, no scenery, no other objects, no floor texture, no labels, no captions, no reference panels, no borders, no watermark. Minimal contact shading immediately at tire contact only. Long-lens/near-orthographic product view; no wide-angle distortion. All visible parts belong to the same single asset.
Critical: no human or rider, no city, no road, no stand-alone decorative props; do not put several views or multiple vehicles into one image. Show one structurally believable object suitable for reconstructing a mesh. Distinguish glass, painted metal, matte plastic and rubber without excessive shininess. No vehicle manufacturer badges, brand text, advertising, readable number plates, or route text.
Subject: the compact WHITE FOUR-DOOR SEDAN category marked T01, inspired by the white commuter cars in the city board's street-level junction and underpass detail. A generic contemporary mainland Chinese family sedan, ordinary practical styling, believable 4.5m-ish proportions, moderate rounded front bumper, slim clear headlamp housings, black lower grille, white bonnet/body/mirrors, four distinct door outlines, simple flush-ish door handles, a conventional boot/trunk, a gentle sedan roofline, subtle blue-gray medium-tinted glazing, clean realistic dark tires and matching simple five-spoke gray alloy wheels. Closed hood, closed trunk and closed doors, no visible engine. Exactly four wheels with correct front/rear wheel arches, no melted body or asymmetric wheel sizes. Blank simple front number-plate recess.
Camera: front-left three-quarter view at a modest elevated angle, about 30 degrees around the front toward the driver's left side and about 10 degrees above the vehicle. Show the complete front fascia, left body side and roof together; front and rear left wheels are clearly separated and legible. Wheels point straight. Square or modest landscape composition, single white sedan only.
